    About CeruSky

    CeruSky is a weather website that shows your local weather, a 36-hour and 7-day forecast, live radar that tells rain from snow and hail, air quality, weather alerts, and national weather news, all in one place.

    I built the foundational layout (frontend) of the project, then used Figma to refine what I already had. After that, I used AI for a lot of the frontend code (a timesaver) so I could focus on learning and building the backend of the application.

    Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with Leaflet for the radar maps.

    Built by Eric Johnson. See the code on GitHub.

    Radar
    NOAA's National Weather Service, Multi-Radar Multi-Sensor (MRMS). CeruSky recolors it to show rain, snow, and hail, so the colors are CeruSky's, not NOAA's.
    Backup radar
    National Weather Service NEXRAD radar from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University, recolored by CeruSky to match the main radar. Its rain and snow are estimated using current temperatures from Open-Meteo. Shown for the first moment while the main radar loads, and whenever the main radar can't load.
    Weather alerts
    National Weather Service
    Maps
    Map tiles from Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and the GIS user community. Shown with Leaflet.
    City photos
    Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Each photographer is credited on the photo when its license asks for it.
    News stories
    Google News, converted with rss2json. Every story links to the site that published it.
    Featured story photos and videos
    FOX Weather, from its news feed and its YouTube channel. Each one links to FOX Weather.
